The Problem

In remote rural areas, access to emergency medical assistance is often delayed due to poor communication, lack of infrastructure, and limited availability of healthcare providers. RemoteCare CLI solves this by streamlining emergency management and communication.

Core Features

  • Log medical emergencies with details like patient name, location, symptoms, and severity level.
  • Prioritize emergencies based on severity (High > Medium > Low).
  • Notify nearby healthcare providers via SMS or email.
  • Provide offline first aid guides for common emergencies.

How to Use

Log Medical Emergencies

remotecare log --name "John Doe" --symptoms "Severe chest pain" --location "Village A" --severity "High"

Prioritize Cases

remotecare prioritize

View First Aid Guide

remotecare guide --topic "burns"

Example Output:

First Aid for Burns: - Cool the burn under running water for at least 10 minutes. - Cover the burn with a clean, non-fluffy cloth. - Do not apply creams or lotions.
