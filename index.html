The Problem

Millions in rural areas struggle with limited healthcare access. Our tool addresses this challenge by providing a user-friendly platform for scheduling teleconsultations, sharing health guidelines, and securely managing patient data.

Tool Features

  • Streamlined scheduling system for healthcare appointments.
  • Access to community-specific health guidelines.
  • Secure patient data sharing compliant with data privacy standards.
  • Log emergencies and prioritize cases (from CLI).
  • Access first aid guides (from CLI).

Pricing Model

Our tool is free for individual use to ensure accessibility in rural areas. Advanced features for organizations, such as reporting and system integrations, are available through a paid subscription.
